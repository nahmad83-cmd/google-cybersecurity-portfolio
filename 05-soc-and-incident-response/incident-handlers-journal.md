# Incident Handler's Journal

## Journal Entry

The training scenario involved a medical services provider affected by ransomware following a phishing attack.

## The 5 W's

- **Who:** A coordinated group of malicious actors.
- **What:** A ransomware incident that encrypted important files across the network.
- **When:** Tuesday at approximately 9:00 a.m.
- **Where:** A medical services provider.
- **Why:** Attackers gained access through phishing and deployed ransomware, apparently seeking financial gain through a ransom demand.

## Follow-Up Questions

The journal recorded questions about preventive controls the organization could implement and how the organization should approach recovery and ransom-related decisions.

## Key Takeaway

A structured incident journal supports clear documentation, preserves important facts, and helps analysts separate confirmed observations from follow-up questions that still require investigation.

## Skills Demonstrated

Incident documentation, ransomware awareness, phishing analysis, the 5 W's, evidence organization, and incident-response communication.
